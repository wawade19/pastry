Our craft

  1. Day one: mix and rest

    Dough is mixed, chilled and left to ferment overnight for flavor.

  2. Day two: fold and shape

    Butter is folded in by hand, rolled, cut and proofed until it wobbles.

  3. Day three: bake

    Trays go in at five, so the first croissants are ready by seven.
